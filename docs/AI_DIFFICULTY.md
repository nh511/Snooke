# AI Difficulty, Safety and Risk

SelectWeightedShot ranks already computed physical evaluations. Offense rewards
legal pots and penalizes cue/illegal pots. Safety is final cue distance from the
nearest active object ball. Candidate cut/path risk and deterministic seeded
direction-error penalty reduce the total. Every term is included in Detail.

Easy emphasizes safety/risk and has larger seeded error; Hard emphasizes pots and
uses zero error. Medium sits between. Identical candidates/profile/seed produce
identical choice and detail. The selected record remains a real shared-service
evaluation and is replay-checked; difficulty never teleports balls or edits scores.
