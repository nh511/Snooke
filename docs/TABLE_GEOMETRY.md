# Table Geometry (DAT-007)

GenerateTableGeometry consumes TableDefinition and versioned cushion/pocket registries. It emits separate table/cloth bounds, four cloth edges, finite rail nose/upper-edge segments, flared end transitions, tessellated pocket jaw arcs, mouths and finite sloped-floor strips. All dimensions come from input data, not scattered table constants. Jaw tessellation and coordinate tolerance are explicit settings. Materials and their versions remain attached to geometry for future consumers.

Table-v1 only declares straight rails on axis-aligned cloth edges and pockets centered on edge/corner boundaries at z=0. Unsupported placement is rejected rather than guessed. Corner pocket inward direction is the normalized sum of its adjacent edge normals. Jaw arcs start at mouth endpoints and curve outward with the configured signed jaw angle. Floor slope is measured downward from the horizontal: positive depth divided by tan(slope) determines outward run. A zero slope cannot reach finite depth and is rejected at geometry generation, though the independent parameter loader can represent it. Fixtures are partial engineering layouts, NOT complete regulation six-pocket geometry.

Cushion height and pocket opening/depth repeated by legacy table-v1 must agree with the referenced material. Missing/duplicate IDs, inconsistent values, overlapping end transitions, invalid settings or arithmetic overflow fail transactionally. Repeated generation and changed configurations are tested. The current CushionTop segment denotes the upper rail edge at declared contact height, not a fabricated 3D rubber mesh or a completed top-face collision model. Full cushion cross-section/3D response remains future physics work.

snooker_table_geometry table-file cushion-profile pocket-profile output.svg renders the generated finite primitives directly. Cyan is rail edge, yellow transitions, pink jaw arcs, white mouth/ball and purple floor projection. The generator does not simulate collisions or award a pot. Generated jaw/floor profiles must be physically calibrated before any realism claim. UE editor mesh generation has not been compiled or tested. Training fixture version table-training-v2 corrects its rail height to match the referenced training cushion profile (0.032 m). The earlier conflict was rejected, not silently resolved.
