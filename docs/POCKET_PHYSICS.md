# Pocket Geometry Development (PHY-018/019)

FindPocketContact sweeps a sphere at constant linear velocity over a supplied horizon against finite tessellated jaws, side lips, two-triangle slope and finite bottom support. Analytic sphere-point, sphere-segment interior and sphere-triangle plane roots cover endpoints/edges/faces without time sampling. It does NOT close the mouth with a trigger wall. Query true with Hit=false means no contact; errors preserve output. Initial overlap is reported and rebound rejects penetration instead of teleporting a ball. Coordinates, velocity and swept displacement must remain in a documented 1e6 SI arithmetic envelope; sphere radius is bounded to [1e-9,1e3] m. Curved/gravity trajectories must be subdivided by a future integrator.

The generated bottom extends outward from the slope foot by twice the configured jaw radius, with configured opening width and depth; it is a simple explicit engineering geometry convention, NOT a measured pocket or hidden scoring trigger. It must be versioned in a full solver/build record when used for real shots. Slope contact is not bottom capture. ReflectPocketContact verifies current geometry contact and applies frictionless normal restitution (zero at bottom, configured jaw value otherwise), preserving spin. No pot/scoring event is produced by geometry or rebound. Tangential pocket friction and gravity-driven pocket traversal remain unimplemented.

Current profile fixtures and tessellation are not physical calibration. A global earliest-event solver, accelerated CCD, pocket/cloth support switching, full material registry/geometry replay packaging and actual UE integration remain separate work. A sphere can rebound from a jaw without being potted; only the subsequent state machine can emit a once-only pot after verified bottom/depth conditions. Until that chain is tested, PHY-018 stays REVIEW rather than claiming its complete pot acceptance.
