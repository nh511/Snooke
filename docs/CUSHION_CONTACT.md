# Cushion Contact Baseline

FindCushionContacts queries the actual finite generated contour segments at the current ball position, with explicit contact tolerance and stable segment index. Cloth-boundary distance is a planar diagnostic only; it never closes a pocket or generates an impulse. Rail nose, upper edge and end transition are classified separately by geometry and center height. The upper edge is NOT a full rubber top surface. Jaws are excluded until PHY-018. This query is not CCD and cannot guarantee against tunneling on its own.

ResolveCushionContact requeries geometry to reject stale or fabricated contact metadata/normal. It resolves only real material-backed contours within contact tolerance, uses normal restitution and capped tangential friction, includes angular contact slip and applies solid-sphere angular impulse. Gross penetration is an error, not a hidden position teleport. Separating contact is a no-op; diagnostic edges are rejected. Material version, valid finite state and nonincreasing total kinetic energy are checked before committing output. Side spin may transfer rotational energy into lateral motion; total energy is the relevant bound.

Tests use actual generated rail candidates, one head-on impact, two analytically timed flights between parallel finite rails, opposite side spins and saved pre/post states with embedded profile bytes. Saved contacts are regenerated and re-solved exactly. This is local contact-response reconstruction, NOT complete ShotRecord/REP-002 replay, chronological multi-contact stepping, cushion CCD, cloth-cushion simultaneous constraints or empirical rail calibration. Top-face cross-section and contact stack handling remain explicit limitations; no UE verification has occurred.
